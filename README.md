Entry for the [Fortune in a Pot](https://make.fun/bounty/u-golden-heron-44-fortune-in-a-pot-ded981e9) bounty on make.fun: one kind, funny fortune for a houseplant, in 120 characters or fewer.

## How it works

`main.py` writes a single fixed fortune to `/output/entry.txt`. The text is a constant in the program, so every run produces byte-identical output — fully deterministic, no randomness, no network access, no environment input.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```

## The fortune

> This week, the sun will find your favourite leaf on the sill, and your keeper will swear you leaned in to meet it.

114 characters, one line, written for the plant on the windowsill: a small prediction, a gentle joke about the keeper who talks to their plant, and nothing but warmth — no doom, no withering, no mockery.
